From a thirty-second ad to money in your UPI app

The full mechanics: where points come from, what they are worth, why some are locked, and exactly what happens when you request a payout.

Short videos, small and steady

Tap the earn button on the home screen and a rewarded video plays. Watch it through to the end — closing it early pays nothing, because the ad network only reports completed views.

  • Each completed view adds points to your available balance within a few seconds.
  • There is a daily cap of 20 rewarded ads per account. The counter on the home screen shows where you are.
  • A short cool-down sits between views. It is there to stop automated scripts, not to slow you down.
  • Ad supply varies by day and region. Some days there will simply be fewer ads to serve you.

What happens after you watch

1

Ad completes

The network records a finished view on its side.

2

Signed callback

It sends our server a signed confirmation. The app is not asked.

3

Checks run

Signature, daily cap and timing are all verified.

4

Points credited

A ledger line appears in your wallet with the source.

Where the real money is

The Earn tab lists tasks from partner networks: surveys, app installs with a few minutes of usage, sign-ups and trials. They take longer than an ad and pay several times more, because advertisers pay far more for a completed action than for a view.

  • Every task card shows its reward and rough time before you start.
  • Follow the task instructions exactly. Partners reject completions that skip steps, and a rejected task pays nothing.
  • Task credits can take minutes to hours, because the partner confirms them on their own schedule.
  • Surveys sometimes screen you out partway. That is the partner’s decision and unfortunately it is unpaid.

Why tasks beat ads

An advertiser pays a small amount for someone to see a video. They pay a lot more for someone to install their app and actually use it, or to finish a survey they can use as research.

If you want your balance to move at a useful pace, treat ads as the daily habit and tasks as the thing that actually builds the total.

Invite people who will actually use it

Your referral code is in the Referral tab, ready to copy or share. A friend enters it while signing up — it cannot be added afterwards.

  • Your referral bonus is created immediately, but stays locked.
  • It unlocks once your friend completes one verified ad view or one partner task.
  • Multiple accounts from the same device or the same person do not count and will flag both accounts.
  • The Referral tab shows who joined, who has activated, and what you have earned from each.

The one rule that matters

Referral rewards are funded by the revenue your friend generates. That is why the unlock is tied to their first verified activity rather than to the signup itself. It is also why referral farming is pointless here — an account that never earns never unlocks anything.

Points, locks and the rupee value

Three numbers matter in your wallet. Here is what each one means and when it changes.

01

Available points

Everything you have earned from verified ads, completed tasks and unlocked bonuses. This is the number that counts towards the payout threshold and the number that gets converted when you withdraw.

02

Locked points

Your signup bonus and any referral bonuses that have not met their unlock condition yet. They are visible, they are yours, and they merge into your available balance automatically once the condition is met. An unused signup bonus expires after 30 days.

03

Rupee value

Shown on the Withdraw screen only. Your available points are multiplied by the current conversion rate, which is stored on our server. The rate in force at the moment of your request is recorded with that payout, so any dispute can be traced exactly.

Getting paid

Payouts go over UPI to an ID you control. Here is the sequence, including the parts that are not instant.

1

Cross the threshold

The Withdraw button unlocks when your available balance passes the current payout minimum. Until then the screen shows how far you have left to go.

2

Enter your UPI ID

Use a UPI ID registered in your own name. Payouts to someone else’s ID are rejected, and repeated attempts get the account flagged.

3

The request is held

Your points are deducted and held against the request so the same balance cannot be withdrawn twice. The request shows as pending in your wallet.

4

Review and payout

We check the account for fraud flags, then send the payment through our payout provider. The status changes to paid with a reference you can match in your bank app.

Good to know before you request

  • One pending withdrawal at a time, per account.
  • Your email must be verified before a payout can be processed.
  • Nothing is ever marked paid before our payout provider confirms the transfer.
  • If a request is rejected, the held points return to your balance with a reason attached.
The threshold can move. It sits in a published range and is set on our side, because it has to stay above the fees our payout provider charges per transfer. The live figure is always shown on the Withdraw screen.
