Why the numbers in your wallet can be trusted

One principle runs through the whole build: the phone in your hand is never the source of truth about money. Every rupee-relevant decision is made and recorded on our server.

Server-verified rewards

Rewarded ads use server-side verification. The ad network signs a callback to our endpoint and we check that signature before a single point is written. A reward request that comes from the app alone is discarded.

Signed task postbacks

Partner networks confirm completed tasks through postbacks carrying a shared secret. We validate the hash and store the raw payload, so a completion can always be re-checked if a partner disputes it.

Locked-down database rules

Your account document is readable only by you, and the fields that hold balances and locked bonuses cannot be written by any client at all. Only backend functions can change them.

How fraud is kept out

Reward apps die when farms of fake accounts drain the pool faster than advertisers refill it. These controls exist so genuine users keep getting paid.

One device, one account

A device identifier is bound to your account at signup. A second signup from the same hardware is blocked or flagged for review rather than silently allowed.

Emulator and root detection

Most automated reward fraud runs on emulators or rooted devices. On a flagged device the app still opens, but earning features are disabled.

Rate limiting

Reward requests are throttled on the server. Humans pause naturally between ads; scripts do not, and that difference is easy to see.

Behavioural review

Sudden earning spikes, accounts that hit exactly the cap every single day, and identical referral patterns go into a manual review queue rather than being auto-banned.

App integrity checks

Backend calls are attested, so requests from a repackaged or tampered build are rejected before they reach any reward logic.

If your account is flagged by mistake, write to codewithkutub@gmail.com. Flags put a hold on earning while we look; they are not an automatic ban, and a cleared account keeps its balance.

Money integrity

  • Every balance change writes an immutable ledger entry. Entries are appended, never edited or deleted.
  • Balances are periodically reconciled against the sum of the ledger, which surfaces any discrepancy quickly.
  • Locked and available balances are separate fields, and the split is enforced on the server at withdrawal time.
  • The withdrawal threshold is re-checked server-side even if the app shows the button as unlocked.
  • Conversion-rate and threshold changes are logged with the old value, the new value, who changed it and when.

Your data

  • We collect an email address, a device identifier used for fraud checks, and a UPI ID at payout time. That is the list.
  • No access to contacts, photos, SMS or call logs is requested, because none of it is needed.
  • We do not sell personal data. Partners receive only what a task requires to confirm your completion.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data from inside the app or by email, in line with the DPDP Act.
  • Transaction records are retained where tax and audit rules require it, even after deletion of the account itself.

Protecting yourself

Most losses in this category come from impersonation rather than from the app itself. Four habits make you a hard target.

  • Download only from this site. Verify the checksum on the download page before installing.
  • Nobody from EarnKit will ever ask for your UPI PIN, an OTP, or a payment to release a withdrawal.
  • Ignore third-party groups selling “unlimited points” mods. They are credential thieves, and using one gets your real account banned.
  • Check that support email replies come from our domain, not a lookalike.

Verified, signed, and yours to inspect

The download page publishes the exact file size, version and SHA-256 checksum so you can confirm what you are installing.
