Frequently asked questions

Earning, payouts, locked bonuses, account limits and data — answered plainly. If something is missing, write to us and we will add it.

Getting started

Is EarnKit free?

Yes, completely. There is no joining fee, no subscription and no charge for withdrawing. If anyone asks you to pay to unlock a reward or speed up a payout, it is not us.

What do I need to sign up?

An email address you can verify and an Android phone running Android 7.0+. A UPI ID is only needed when you request your first withdrawal.

Can I use it on more than one phone?

You can sign in to your account on a different phone, but you cannot hold a second account. Accounts are bound to a device at signup and duplicate signups are blocked or flagged.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not currently. The app is Android only, and any iOS build claiming to be ours is fake.

Earning

How much can I earn per day?

It depends on how many rewarded ads are available to you and which partner tasks you finish. We do not publish a rupees-per-day number because ad rates move constantly and it would be a guess. Tasks pay several times more than ads, so they make the biggest difference.

Why did my points not increase after watching an ad?

The ad has to complete for the network to report it, and the credit is written only after our server verifies that report. Closing an ad early, losing connection midway, or hitting the daily cap will all result in no credit. Check your ledger first — credits usually land within seconds.

Why is there a daily limit on ads?

Ad networks cap how many rewarded views one person can be paid for in a day. Going past it would mean paying you for views we are not paid for, which is how reward apps run out of money. The counter on the home screen shows your remaining ads.

A task I completed was not credited.

Partner tasks are confirmed by the partner, not by us, and some take hours to clear. If a task is still missing after 72 hours, email support with the task name, the time you finished it and a screenshot of the completion.

Points and bonuses

What is an EarnKit Point worth?

Points convert at a rate held on our server, and the rupee value of your balance is shown on the Withdraw screen. The rate in force when you request a payout is recorded against that payout.

Why is part of my balance locked?

Signup and referral bonuses are funded by real ad and task revenue, so they unlock once you have earned enough yourself. Your wallet shows the unlock target and the merge happens automatically.

What happens if my signup bonus expires?

An unused signup bonus expires 30 days after signup. Any points you earned yourself are unaffected — they never expire.

When does a referral bonus unlock?

After the person you referred completes their first verified ad view or partner task. A signup alone does not unlock it, and referrals from the same device do not count.

Withdrawals

When can I withdraw?

Once your available balance passes the payout threshold shown on the Withdraw screen. Locked points do not count towards it.

How long does a payout take?

Requests go into review before being sent through our payout provider. Most clear within a few working days. The status in your wallet moves from pending to paid with a reference number.

Can I withdraw to someone else's UPI ID?

No. The UPI ID must belong to you. Payouts to third-party IDs are rejected and repeated attempts flag the account.

My withdrawal was rejected. What now?

The held points return to your available balance with a reason attached. The usual causes are an unverified email, an incorrect UPI ID, or an open fraud flag. Fix the cause and request again.

Account and data

Why was my account flagged?

Automatic checks look for emulators, rooted devices, duplicate accounts on one device and abnormal earning speed. A flag pauses earning while a human reviews it. If it clears, your balance is intact.

What data do you collect?

An email address, a device identifier used for fraud checks, your activity inside the app, and a UPI ID when you withdraw. We do not request contacts, photos, SMS or call logs.

How do I delete my account?

Request deletion from the Profile screen or by email. We remove your personal data, though transaction records are retained where tax and audit rules require it.

How do I contact support?

Email codewithkutub@gmail.com. Include the email address on your account, and for a missing reward include the date, time and a screenshot.

Still stuck?

Email us with your account address and, if it is about a missing reward, the date and a screenshot. It makes the answer much faster.
