Terms of use — EarnKit

The rules for accounts, earning activity, withdrawals, fraud prevention and use of the EarnKit service.

Eligibility and accounts

You must provide accurate account information and keep your login credentials secure. EarnKit permits one account per person and may bind an account to a device or security identity for fraud prevention.

Use the service only where you are legally permitted to do so.

Earning rules

Points are credited only for activity that our systems and relevant advertising or task partners can verify. Closing ads early, using automation, manipulating traffic, modifying the app or attempting to manufacture activity can result in rejected rewards or account restrictions.

Reward availability, daily limits, task inventory, conversion rates and payout thresholds may change. Current values shown inside the service are the values that apply at the relevant time.

Withdrawals

Withdrawals are subject to the available-balance threshold, verification checks and payout-provider requirements. The UPI ID submitted must belong to you.

Requests can be delayed, rejected or reversed when verification fails, required information is inaccurate, or activity is determined to be invalid.

Prohibited use

Do not create multiple accounts, use emulators or automation to generate rewards, tamper with the application, interfere with servers or APIs, exploit bugs, submit false information, or use another person's payment details.

Service availability

EarnKit may change, suspend or discontinue features for maintenance, security, partner availability or legal reasons. We will make reasonable efforts to communicate material service changes.

Contact

For questions about these terms, contact codewithkutub@gmail.com. This product-level terms template should be reviewed by qualified counsel before public production use.
