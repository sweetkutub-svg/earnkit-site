Privacy policy — EarnKit

How EarnKit collects, uses, protects and deletes account, earning and payout information.

Information we collect

We collect the information needed to operate an EarnKit account, including your email address, account identifiers, device and security signals, wallet activity, transaction history and the UPI ID you provide for a payout.

We may receive event and attribution information from advertising and task partners so completed activity can be verified and credited correctly.

How we use information

We use account and activity data to provide the service, verify rewards, prevent duplicate or fraudulent accounts, process withdrawals, maintain the transaction ledger, troubleshoot issues and communicate important service changes.

Reward availability and payout timing can vary based on advertising and partner activity.

Sharing

We share information only when reasonably required with service providers that support authentication, analytics, advertising, task verification, fraud prevention and payouts. We do not sell personal information as a product.

Third-party partners may process information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

We retain records as reasonably necessary to operate the service, maintain security, resolve disputes and meet legal or accounting obligations. You may request account and data deletion through account controls or by contacting support.

Some transaction, fraud-prevention or compliance records may need to be retained after account deletion where required.

Security

We use access controls, server-side verification and other technical and organizational measures designed to protect account and transaction data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact codewithkutub@gmail.com. This product-level notice should be reviewed by qualified counsel before production use in jurisdictions with specific legal requirements.
