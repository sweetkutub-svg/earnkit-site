Everything in the app, and why it is there

No feature exists to make a screenshot look busy. Each one solves a specific problem for either you or the health of the reward pool.

Live wallet

Available and locked points side by side, with the unlock target spelled out. The balance animates up when a credit lands, so you can see the moment it happens.

Daily ad counter

A visible progress bar towards the daily cap. You always know how many rewarded videos are left rather than finding out by being refused.

Offerwall of tasks

Partner surveys, installs and trials with the reward and time estimate on every card, sortable so the highest paying ones are easy to find.

Full transaction ledger

Every credit and debit, with source, amount and timestamp. Filter by type when you want to see just withdrawals or just referral income.

Referral dashboard

Your code, one-tap share, and a list of everyone who joined with their activation status so you can see which bonuses are still locked.

UPI withdrawals

Points converted at the live rate, a clear rupee figure before you confirm, and status tracking from pending through to paid.

Status notifications

Alerts for a bonus unlocking, a task finally clearing, and every change to a withdrawal request. All of them can be turned off.

Dark mode

A proper dark theme, not an inverted light one. It follows your system setting by default and can be pinned either way.

Account controls

Verify your email, update your UPI ID, see your fraud-check status and request deletion of your account and data from inside the app.

What we deliberately left out

Reward apps usually fail for predictable reasons. These omissions are decisions, not gaps in the roadmap.

Spin wheels and scratch cards

Random cash prizes can be treated as gambling under Indian rules and they make earnings unpredictable. You are paid for a completed action, every time.

Rupees-per-day promises

Ad rates change weekly and vary by region. Any app quoting you a guaranteed daily figure is either guessing or planning not to pay it.

Unlock fees and deposits

You will never be asked to pay to withdraw, to verify, or to raise your level. There is no mechanism in the app that takes money from you.

Contacts and gallery access

We ask for your email and, at payout time, your UPI ID. We do not need your contact list or your photos, so we do not request them.

Under the hood

For the technically curious, this is the stack the app runs on.

App

Flutter, built for Android and distributed as a signed APK. One codebase, native performance, and a release channel we control directly.

Backend

Firebase Authentication, Firestore and Cloud Functions. All money logic — crediting, capping, unlocking, withdrawing — runs in functions the client cannot call into directly.

Ads and tasks

Google AdMob rewarded video with server-side verification, plus offerwall partners that confirm completions through signed postbacks to our endpoint.

Payouts

A licensed Indian payout provider over UPI, using a registered business account. We never handle transfers manually.

Integrity

App Check on every backend call, device binding at signup, emulator and root detection, and server-side rate limiting on reward requests.

See it running on your own phone

The APK is signed and the checksum is published. Install it, look around, and uninstall any time without leaving anything behind.
