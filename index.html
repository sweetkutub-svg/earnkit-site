Four steps, start to payout

Nothing hidden behind a paywall and nothing to pay upfront. You earn, the balance builds, and you withdraw when it crosses the threshold.

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email and verify it. If a friend sent you a referral code, enter it here — it only works at signup. A welcome bonus lands in your wallet as locked points.

2

Watch ads and finish tasks

Tap Watch ad & earn for a short rewarded video, or open the Earn tab for partner tasks like surveys and app trials, which pay considerably more per action.

3

Points get verified, then credited

The ad network confirms the view to our server before anything touches your balance. You see the credit appear in your ledger with its source and timestamp.

4

Withdraw to UPI

Once your balance crosses the payout threshold, the Withdraw screen unlocks and shows your points converted to rupees. Enter your UPI ID and the request goes into review.

What an EarnKit Point actually is

A point is a unit of the ad and task revenue your activity generated, held in your wallet until you withdraw. We show points instead of rupees in the app because a single ad is worth a fraction of a rupee, and a number like 18 is easier to read than ₹0.18.

  • The points-to-rupees rate lives on our server, never inside the app, so it can never be tampered with from a modified build.
  • The rupee value of your balance is shown on the Withdraw screen, and the rate used is recorded with every payout.
  • If the rate changes, the change is logged and applies from that moment onward — it is never applied backwards to points you already hold.

Built for people who have been burned before

This category has a reputation problem. These are the specific rules the app runs on, and they are enforced by the server, not by the screen you are looking at.

Nothing credits from the phone

A reward is only written after the ad network independently confirms to our server that the view was genuine. Modified apps and automation tools have nothing to exploit.

An honest daily ceiling

Ad networks cap how many rewarded videos one person can be paid for in a day. We show you the counter instead of pretending the supply is endless.

Your ledger is append-only

Every credit and debit is a permanent line with its source, amount and timestamp. Balances are reconciled against that history, so numbers cannot quietly change.

One account per device

Device checks block the multi-account farms that drain reward apps and get them shut down. Real users keep earning because fake ones do not.

Withdrawals are re-checked

Even if the button looks unlocked, the server re-verifies your real balance against the current threshold before a payout request is accepted.

No luck, no gambling

No spin wheels, scratch cards or random jackpots. You are paid for work you actually did, which also keeps the app on the right side of Indian gaming rules.

Questions people ask first

Is there any catch or joining fee?

No. Installing is free, earning is free and withdrawing is free. We are paid by advertisers for the ad views and task completions you generate, and you get a share of that. Anyone asking you to pay to unlock a reward is not us.

How much can I actually earn?

It depends on how many rewarded ads are available to you on a given day and which partner tasks you complete — tasks pay far more than ads. We deliberately do not print a rupees-per-day number, because it would vary by person and would be a guess.

Why is my bonus locked?

Signup and referral bonuses are funded by real revenue, so they unlock once you have earned enough on your own. Your wallet shows the unlock target, and the merge into your available balance is automatic.

Why is the app not on the Play Store?

EarnKit is currently available on Xiaomi GetApps and as a signed APK from this site, while the app is in its early release. The download page explains how to verify the file and install it safely.
